# Further study

The examples and explanations in this repository are original teaching material.
These primary references provide deeper derivations and physical context:

- [MIT OpenCourseWare — Mechanics of Materials, David Roylance](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-11-mechanics-of-materials-fall-1999/): stress, strain, torsion, bending, and the connection between material behavior and structures.
- [MIT mechanics modules](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-11-mechanics-of-materials-fall-1999/pages/modules/): modular reading to accompany the fundamentals.
- [AFGROW Damage Tolerant Design Handbook, Section 5.1.2](https://www.afgrow.net/applications/DTDHandbook/sections/page5_1_2.aspx): crack-growth models, experimental coefficients, and applicability limits of the Paris relation.

Reference links were checked during preparation. No material allowables, S-N
curves, or fatigue coefficients were extracted from these pages. Replace the
synthetic baseline properties only with a documented, consistent dataset.
